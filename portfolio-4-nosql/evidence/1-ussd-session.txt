Server: 8.2.5 (save: 3600 1 300 100 60 10000, appendonly: no)

=== Key press 1: Halima dials *347*8#. A session hash is created ===
> HSET tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 msisdn +2348031110003 account 2010000038 menu MAIN
(integer) 3
> EXPIRE tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 180
(integer) 1
> TTL tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91
(integer) 180

=== Key press 2: she chooses 1 (Send money) ===
> HSET tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 menu ENTER_RECIPIENT
(integer) 0
> EXPIRE tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 180
(integer) 1

=== Key press 3: she enters the recipient's account number ===
> HSET tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 menu ENTER_AMOUNT recipient 2010000054
(integer) 1
> EXPIRE tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 180
(integer) 1

=== Two seconds later the session is still alive, and its timer counts down ===
> TTL tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91
(integer) 178
> HGETALL tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91
1) "msisdn"
2) "+2348031110003"
3) "account"
4) "2010000038"
5) "menu"
6) "ENTER_AMOUNT"
7) "recipient"
8) "2010000054"
Each key press reads and updates one small hash by its key: no table scan, no join.

=== Key press 4: she enters 5000 and confirms with her PIN ===
> HSET tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91 menu CONFIRM amount 5000
(integer) 1
TosiPay now calls post_transfer() in PostgreSQL (Portfolio 3), then ends the session:
> DEL tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91
(integer) 1
> EXISTS tosipay:ussd:ATUid_7f3c91
(integer) 0

=== An abandoned session: the customer walks away in the middle of the menu ===
> HSET tosipay:ussd:ATUid_a210be msisdn +2348031110005 account 2010000054 menu ENTER_AMOUNT
(integer) 3
The demo uses a 3 second TTL instead of 180 seconds, so it does not have to wait 3 minutes.
> EXPIRE tosipay:ussd:ATUid_a210be 3
(integer) 1
> EXISTS tosipay:ussd:ATUid_a210be
(integer) 0
Redis removed the session by itself when the TTL ran out. No clean-up job or DELETE was needed.
